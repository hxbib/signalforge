## Experiment A · does a listing describe the exact same product?

Independent inputs we did not write or label: 40 listing pairs drawn blind from the **WDC Product Data Corpus v2** non-normalized gold standard (Peeters, Primpeli, Bizer; https://webdatacommons.org/largescaleproductcorpus/v2/), computers + cameras, 10 matching and 10 non-matching pairs per category. Models see only title, description, brand and spec text; ids, clusters and labels never enter a model input. The system arm is the same two-model listing check the live app uses to decide whether a merchant listing is the photographed item (identity tier `model_number`, `app.models.pair`). Abstentions and errors count as incorrect and stay in the denominator.

No case, price or score was substituted.

- A sampling frame from an authorized merchant or manufacturer catalog whose terms grant retention and offsite model-evaluation rights. The shop.dev catalog is used shopper-triggered with a short eligibility window; spec/RETAILERS.md forbids feeding restricted external offer data to an evaluation job, and no source in the registry records an evaluation right.
- Five rights-cleared product images (merchant-licensed or the team's own new capture of each drawn item).
