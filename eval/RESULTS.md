## Experiment A · does a listing describe the exact same product?

Independent inputs we did not write or label: two blind draws of 40 listing pairs each (draw A, and held-out draw B disjoint from A) from the **WDC Product Data Corpus v2** non-normalized gold standard (Peeters, Primpeli, Bizer; https://webdatacommons.org/largescaleproductcorpus/v2/), computers + cameras, 10 matching and 10 non-matching pairs per category. ## Five-basket photo-to-offer shopping check (protocol Experiment B)

Status: **not_run**. The five-case photo-to-offer check (eval/seal_baskets.py; baseline basket_answer(packet, False) vs system basket_answer(packet, True)) was not run: rights-cleared merchant evidence for five preregistered cases could not be assembled before the 16:05 ET proof cutoff. No case, price or score was substituted.

- A sampling frame from an authorized merchant or manufacturer catalog whose terms grant retention and offsite model-evaluation rights. The shop.dev catalog is used shopper-triggered with a short eligibility window; spec/RETAILERS.md forbids feeding restricted external offer data to an evaluation job, and no source in the registry records an evaluation right.
- Five rights-cleared product images (merchant-licensed or the team's own new capture of each drawn item).
- A human operator adjudication pass of all five cases (GTIN/model, pack, seller, stock, basis, fulfillment, amount, fees) against the independent source before either arm runs.
- Sealed cases.jsonl with source_rights and gold_evidence per case (eval/seal_baskets.py refuses to seal without them).
